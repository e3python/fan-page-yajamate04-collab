About Ivan Cornejo

He blends regional Mexican styles with modern influences and became known for a soft, emotional sound that mixes sierreño guitars with contemporary storytelling.

Early Life & Musical Beginnings

He taught himself guitar by watching tutorials, started writing songs from personal experiences, and gained attention online at a young age.

Style & Impact

His music is often described as "sad sierreño"—melancholic, guitar-centered, and emotionally direct, which has resonated with younger listeners and helped modernize regional Mexican sounds.

Touring, Popularity & Legacy

His live shows are known for strong fan connection and honest performances. He has built a large following on platforms like TikTok and Spotify and is already a key voice in contemporary música Mexicana.
